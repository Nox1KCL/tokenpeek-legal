FAQ

Common questions.

Does TokenPeek ever send my webpages to a server?

Never. All CSS inspection, Tailwind v4 conversion, token extraction, and PDF generation run 100% locally in your browser. The only optional network request happens when you activate, validate, or deactivate a Pro license key via our stateless proxy to Creem. Read our Privacy Policy.

How do I activate TokenPeek Pro?

No TokenPeek account is required. After checkout on Creem, copy your license key from the receipt page or confirmation email. Open any webpage in Chrome, click the TokenPeek toolbar icon, click Pro, paste your key, and press Activate.

How do I move my license to another browser or PC?

Open TokenPeek on your current browser, click PRO ✓, and click Deactivate on this browser. This immediately frees your device slot on Creem so you can activate the same key on your new PC or Chrome profile. Lost a device? Email noxielabs@gmail.com and we will release the slot.

What is your refund policy?

Every Lifetime Solo ($29) and Lifetime Team ($79) purchase includes a straightforward 14-day money-back guarantee. If TokenPeek Pro isn’t right for your workflow, contact noxielabs@gmail.com with your Creem order ID within 14 days for a full refund (refunded keys are automatically disabled).
